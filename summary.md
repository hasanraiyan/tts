Kabhi tumne notice kiya hai ki tumhe pata hota hai ki tumhe kya karna chahiye, phir bhi tum wahi kaam nahi karte? Tumhe pata hai ki padhna chahiye, lekin phone uth jaata hai. Tumhe pata hai ki project complete karna hai, lekin tum baar baar postpone karte ho. Tumhe pata hai ki ek unhealthy habit chhodni hai, phir bhi tum usi pattern mein wapas chale jaate ho. Aur sabse strange baat yeh hai ki koi bahar ka person tumhe rok nahi raha. Tum khud hi apne goals ke raste mein khade ho. Brianna Wiest ki book The Mountain Is You isi idea ko explore karti hai. Book ke according, life mein sabse difficult mountain kabhi-kabhi bahar nahi hota. Woh mountain humare andar hota hai — humare fears, habits, emotional patterns, insecurities aur self-sabotaging behaviours ka combination. Aur jab tak hum yeh nahi samajhte ki hum khud ko kyun rok rahe hain, tab tak sirf motivation se kuch permanently change nahi hota.

Self-sabotage ka matlab jaan-bujhkar apni life kharab karna nahi hai. Usually hum consciously failure nahi chahte. Problem yeh hai ki humare kuch behaviours ek hidden purpose serve kar rahe hote hain. Ho sakta hai tum procrastinate isliye nahi kar rahe kyunki tum lazy ho. Ho sakta hai difficult task start karne se tumhe anxiety hoti ho, aur procrastination tumhe temporarily us anxiety se relief deta ho. Tumhe lagta hai tum kaam avoid kar rahe ho, lekin tumhara mind actually discomfort avoid kar raha hota hai. Yahi reason hai ki sirf yeh kehna, “Mujhe disciplined banna hai,” har baar enough nahi hota. Tumhe samajhna padta hai ki tumhara current behaviour tumhe kya emotional reward de raha hai.

Imagine karo ek student ko exam ke liye padhna hai. Woh book kholta hai, difficult chapter dekhta hai, anxiety feel karta hai aur phone check kar leta hai. Phone use karne se problem solve nahi hoti, lekin anxiety temporarily kam ho jaati hai. Brain learn karta hai: difficult feeling aayi, phone use karo, relief mil gaya. Agar yeh pattern repeat hota rahe, toh procrastination ek habit ban sakti hai. Ab student khud ko lazy bolta hai, guilt feel karta hai, aur phir aur stress hota hai. Next time difficult task aata hai, woh phir phone ki taraf bhaagta hai. Ek cycle create ho jaati hai.

Isliye book ke perspective se self-sabotage ko sirf discipline ki problem samajhna incomplete ho sakta hai. Hume apne patterns ko curiosity ke saath dekhna chahiye. Jab main koi behaviour repeat karta hoon jo mujhe long term mein hurt karta hai, us moment mein main actually kya feel kar raha hoon? Main kis discomfort se bach raha hoon? Mujhe kis cheez ka fear hai? Aur yeh behaviour mujhe short term mein kya de raha hai?

Yeh questions uncomfortable ho sakte hain, lekin change yahin se start hota hai.

Book ka ek important idea hai ki humari nervous system aur emotional patterns familiar cheezon ki taraf attract ho sakte hain. Familiar ka matlab hamesha healthy nahi hota. Agar tum years se stress mein rahe ho, toh calm environment bhi strange feel ho sakta hai. Agar tum criticism ke aadat mein ho, toh genuine appreciation accept karna difficult lag sakta hai. Agar tum chaos ke used to ho, toh stability boring lag sakti hai. Hum kabhi-kabhi consciously better life chahte hain, lekin unconsciously familiar emotional state ko repeat karte rehte hain.

Isi wajah se change sirf goal set karne se nahi aata. Goal tumhe batata hai ki tum kahan jaana chahte ho. Lekin tumhare patterns batate hain ki tum abhi kis direction mein automatically ja rahe ho.

Maan lo tum kehte ho, “Mujhe successful banna hai.” Lekin jab opportunity aati hai, tum hesitate karte ho. Tum project start nahi karte. Tum apne kaam ko unnecessarily perfect banane ki koshish karte ho. Tum publish karne se darte ho. Bahar se yeh laziness ya perfectionism lag sakta hai. Lekin deeper level par ho sakta hai ki success ke saath responsibility, judgement aur expectations ka fear connected ho. Failure painful hai, lekin success bhi change lekar aata hai. Aur kabhi-kabhi hum change se hi darte hain.

Yeh ek fascinating point hai: hum sirf failure se nahi darte. Kabhi-kabhi hum success se bhi darte hain.

Agar tum successful ho gaye, toh log tumse aur expect karenge. Agar tum apni dream life build kar loge, toh tumhe usse maintain karna padega. Agar tum apni identity change karoge, toh purane relationships aur habits bhi change ho sakte hain. Isliye mind kabhi-kabhi familiar struggle ko unfamiliar success se safer samajh sakta hai.

Iska solution khud ko blame karna nahi hai. Solution awareness hai.

Tum apne behaviour ko observe karo jaise ek scientist experiment observe karta hai. Jab tum procrastinate karte ho, sirf yeh mat bolo, “Main lazy hoon.” Poochho, “Isse just pehle kya hua tha?” Jab tum kisi opportunity se peeche hato, poochho, “Mujhe exactly kis cheez ka fear hai?” Jab tum kisi healthy habit ko todte ho, poochho, “Us moment mein mujhe kya chahiye tha?”

Aise questions tumhe behaviour ke surface se neeche le jaate hain.

Book emotional awareness par bhi emphasis karti hai. Humari feelings enemies nahi hain. Anxiety, anger, sadness, jealousy, fear — yeh signals ho sakte hain. Problem tab hoti hai jab hum in feelings ko samajhne ke bajaye immediately react karte hain.

Anger aaya, toh hum kisi par shout kar dete hain. Anxiety aayi, toh task avoid kar dete hain. Loneliness aayi, toh hum kisi unhealthy distraction mein chale jaate hain. Fear aaya, toh opportunity reject kar dete hain.

Emotion aur action ke beech ek small gap create karna powerful ho sakta hai.

Tumhari feeling real ho sakti hai, lekin har feeling tumhe action ka order nahi deti.

Yeh concept especially important hai kyunki change uncomfortable hota hai. Jab tum old habit todte ho, tumhara brain instantly reward nahi deta. Tumhe resistance feel ho sakta hai. Iska matlab yeh nahi ki tum wrong direction mein ho. Kabhi-kabhi discomfort simply iska signal hota hai ki tum familiar pattern se bahar aa rahe ho.

Socho tum daily exercise start karte ho. Pehle kuch din uncomfortable feel hoga. Agar tum har discomfort ko signal samjhoge ki “shayad mujhe nahi karna chahiye,” toh habit build nahi hogi. Lekin agar tum discomfort ko tolerate karna seekh jao, toh gradually new pattern normal banne lagta hai.

Yahi principle life ke bahut saare areas mein apply hota hai.

Tum public speaking se darte ho? Thoda-thoda exposure lo.

Tum coding project start karne se darte ho? Perfect plan ke wait ke bina small version build karo.

Tum difficult conversation avoid karte ho? Respectfully conversation start karna seekho.

Tum rejection se darte ho? Rejection ko personal identity banane ke bajaye information ki tarah process karo.

Growth ka matlab fear eliminate karna nahi hai.

Growth ka matlab fear ke saath action lena seekhna hai.

Book ka mountain metaphor isi liye powerful hai. Mountain tumhari problem bhi hai aur tumhara path bhi. Jab tum mountain climb karte ho, tum sirf destination tak nahi pahunch rahe hote. Climbing ke process mein tum stronger, more aware aur more capable ban rahe hote ho.

Life mein hum aksar kehte hain, “Meri problem solve ho jaaye, phir main happy rahunga.” Lekin har solved problem ke baad next challenge aa sakta hai. Isliye goal sirf problems eliminate karna nahi ho sakta. Goal yeh bhi hai ki tum challenges handle karne ki capacity develop karo.

Aur yeh capacity small actions se build hoti hai.

Tum ek giant life transformation overnight nahi karoge. Tum ek habit ko identify karoge. Uska trigger samjhoge. Uske emotional reward ko notice karoge. Uske badle ek healthier response create karoge. Phir us response ko repeatedly practice karoge.

For example, agar tum stress mein social media scroll karte ho, toh simply app delete karna ek solution ho sakta hai, lekin deeper question yeh hai: stress ke moment mein tum actually kya need kar rahe ho? Rest? Connection? Escape? Stimulation? Agar underlying need samajh aa jaaye, toh tum healthier alternative create kar sakte ho.

Maybe tumhe five-minute walk chahiye.

Maybe kisi friend se baat karni hai.

Maybe tumhe genuinely rest karna hai.

Maybe task ko smaller steps mein divide karna hai.

Self-sabotage ko replace karne ke liye tumhe apni real needs samajhni padengi.

Book ek aur important lesson deti hai: apni identity ko consciously rebuild karo. Agar tum repeatedly khud se kehte ho, “Main lazy hoon,” “Main disciplined nahi hoon,” “Main unlucky hoon,” toh tumhari identity aur behaviour ek loop bana sakte hain.

Iske instead, identity ko behaviour ke through build karo.
